View size

RAM warning levels: 50% noticing, 70% uneasy, 85% "!" sign, 95% "!!" danger. Gadget size limits: width 220–400, yard height 56–130 (default 280 × 80). One line view: drag its right end to resize sideways, up to its full content. Narrower than that, session names shorten first ("Hou 2"), then Houdini values drop one at a time from the end (a session's RAM, then its CPU). At the smallest only CPU, GPU and RAM remain. While you drag, the bar follows the pointer; when you let go, it glides in to hug what it shows. Reset size makes it fit its content again. Use Actual size to see a long line in full. In this prototype the pet dozes off after 5 seconds of idle (1 minute in the real tool). The yard sky follows your current time of day.
